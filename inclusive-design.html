Microsoft Inclusive Design

A methodology that enables and draws on the full range of human diversity

Project Overview

Microsoft Inclusive Design is a methodology born out of digital environments that enables and draws on the full range of human diversity. Most importantly, this means including and learning from people with a range of perspectives.

As a key contributor to Microsoft's Inclusive Design initiatives, I helped develop principles, tools, and resources that guide teams in creating more accessible and inclusive products. This work has influenced design practices across Microsoft and beyond, helping to ensure that technology serves the needs of all users, regardless of their abilities or circumstances.

Inclusive Design Principles

01

Recognize Exclusion

We acknowledge bias and recognize exclusions that happen because of mismatches between people and experiences. By identifying these exclusions, we can turn them into opportunities for innovation.

02

Learn from Diversity

Inclusive Design puts people in the center throughout the process. Their fresh, diverse perspectives are the key to true insight. We engage with people who have different perspectives to learn from their experiences.

03

Solve for One, Extend to Many

Everyone has abilities and limits. Creating products for people with permanent disabilities creates results that benefit everyone. Solutions that work well for people with permanent disabilities often work well for everyone.

04

Inclusive Design is for You

Whether you're a program manager, engineer, data scientist, designer, or anyone else who helps create products and services, Inclusive Design is a practice you can follow to create more accessible and usable solutions.

Challenges & Solutions

Cognitive Accessibility

Challenge: Many digital experiences fail to account for the diversity of human cognition, creating barriers for users with different cognitive abilities.

Solution: We developed the Inclusive Design for Cognition framework to help teams understand and design for cognitive diversity, including attention, memory, and executive function.

AI Bias

Challenge: AI systems often reflect and amplify existing biases, leading to exclusionary experiences for marginalized groups.

Solution: Our "In Pursuit of Inclusive AI" primer provides guidance on recognizing and mitigating bias in AI systems, helping teams create more equitable AI experiences.

Digital Interruptions

Challenge: Modern digital experiences frequently interrupt users, creating particular challenges for people with attention-related disabilities.

Solution: The "Respecting Focus" guide helps teams design intelligent systems that respect user attention and minimize disruptive interruptions.

Physical Accessibility

Challenge: Physical products often have fixed forms that don't accommodate the full range of human abilities and body types.

Solution: Our "Devices, Accessories, and Augmentations" resource guides teams in designing adjustable and adaptable physical products that meet diverse needs.

Results & Impact

Industry Recognition

The Inclusive Design Principles won the 2017 IXDA Best of Empowering category award, establishing Microsoft as a leader in inclusive design.

Global Adoption

Microsoft's Inclusive Design methodology has been adopted by organizations worldwide, influencing product development across industries.

Cultural Shift

The principles have driven a cultural shift within Microsoft, with inclusive design practices now integrated into product development processes across the company.

More Accessible Products

Microsoft products have become more accessible and usable for people with diverse abilities, leading to improved experiences for all users.
