About Me

Hello, I'm Doug Kim

I'm an Enterprise AI design and product leader, skilled in building effective, highly collaborative teams focused on designing for the era of generative AI. I bring inclusive design practices to bridge the gap between complex AI technologies and intuitive user experiences.

I founded and lead a multidisciplinary team that incorporates the principles of inclusive design with pioneering practices in research, product management, content design, and product design. I've played key roles in lasting innovations like the Microsoft Voice Principles, Inclusive Design at Microsoft, search-engine-based and AI-based help for Windows 10, Xbox, Microsoft 365, and now Copilot in Azure.
